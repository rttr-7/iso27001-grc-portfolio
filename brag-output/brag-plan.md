# Brag Plan: Information Security · GRC · ISO/IEC 27001 Portfolio (Amay Chivate)

## What is this app?
A portfolio and interactive dashboard showing a simulated ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS implementation for Halcyon Ledger Inc., from no ISMS to certification in 11 months.

## The angle
A security-and-compliance case study told as a short financial-results film: money on hold, risk brought under appetite, controls decided, audit passed, certified. Every number comes from the dashboard.

## Hook
"$4.2m" slams in on white, then "of annual recurring revenue on hold".

## Key moments
- Risk heat map: six risks above appetite (score 12+) move into the green. Counter 6 -> 0.
- Annex A grid: 93 cells fill, 91 navy (applicable), 2 grey (excluded with evidence).
- Internal audit: 0 major, 3 minor nonconformities, all closed with root cause analysis.

## Outro
Navy card: "Certified." 16 Oct 2026, 11 months. Amay Chivate, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Auditor. Dashboard URL. "Simulated case study" footer.

## User flow worth showing
Dashboard sections in order: overview -> risk heat map -> Statement of Applicability -> internal audit -> certification.
